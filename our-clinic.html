Our Ottawa Clinic

Where healing begins

Every detail of our clinic space, from the waiting room to the private therapy offices, has been intentionally designed to feel calm, warm, and safe.

Located at 111 Colonnade Road North in Ottawa, our clinic was designed with one goal in mind: to feel like the opposite of a clinical space. Warm wood tones, soft textures, and quiet corners create an environment where you can exhale the moment you walk in, whether you're here for your first session or your fiftieth.

"When we designed our logo, I wanted it to mean something. The hand you see is mine, a smaller hand held in a larger one, a quiet symbol of what this practice is built on: being there to hold space and support someone through their journey."
Sylvia Corzato, Founder
